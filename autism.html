Understanding My Autism

Autism is a neurodevelopmental condition that affects how I perceive and interact with the world. It influences my communication, social interactions, and behavior in unique ways.

Having autism means that I may experience sensory sensitivities, have specific interests or routines, and may find certain social situations challenging. However, it also allows me to have unique perspectives and strengths.

It's important to recognize that autism is a spectrum, and each individual's experience can vary widely. I may have different needs and abilities compared to others, and it's essential to approach interactions with understanding and empathy.

By embracing my autism and seeking support when needed, I can navigate the world in a way that works best for me while also contributing my unique talents and perspectives to society.
